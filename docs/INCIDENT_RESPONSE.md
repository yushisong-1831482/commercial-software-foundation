# Incident response

1. Assign an incident lead, record start time, affected services, scope and severity; restrict access to incident evidence.
2. Stop unsafe rollouts. Contain compromised identities with authorized operators. Preserve redacted audit evidence and avoid destroying forensic data.
3. Assess customer/data impact; involve privacy and legal advisers for notification obligations and deadlines. Do not invent a universal notification deadline.
4. Roll back only when compatible with stored data; destructive recovery or production changes need explicit approval. Restore verified backups into isolation before a production recovery decision.
5. Validate customer journeys, security boundaries and monitoring after recovery. Communicate confirmed facts through approved contacts.
6. Record cause, timeline, corrective actions, owners and regression tests. Review the runbook after drills and incidents.

Escalation contacts, recovery objectives and notification process must be assigned per product before launch.
